About Books & Beyond

Books & Beyond was created to capture the warmth and charm of independent bookstores. Inspired by quiet reading corners and shelves filled with stories, our goal is to create a welcoming place where readers can discover new books and connect with a community that loves literature. From curated selections to cozy events, Books & Beyond celebrates the magic that stories bring into everyday life.

Meet the Founder

Books & Beyond was founded by a passionate reader and author who believed that books have the power to connect people and inspire imagination. Mary, mother of four has dedicated her life to writing and her novel was awarded the best seller in 2015. Her vision was simple: create a warm, cozy bookstore where readers can feel at home, discover new stories, and share their love of literature with others. Today the store continues to bring readers together through thoughtful recommendations, community events, and a shared appreciation for storytelling.
