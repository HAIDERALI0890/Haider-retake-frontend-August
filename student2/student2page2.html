The Midnight Library

by Matt Haig

Between life and death there is a library filled with books containing different versions of your life. Nora Seed gets the chance to explore what might have happened if she had made different choices.

A powerful novel about regret, hope, and discovering the meaning of life. Perfect for readers who enjoy reflective and emotional storytelling.
